Take control of an airline and build it into a global network with Airline Investor.

Airline Investor is a single-player management game played on a map of the world. Found your airline, choose its headquarters, and decide where it flies next. Every route you open, every aircraft you buy, and every city you serve shapes how your airline grows.

Plan your network on a living world map:

- Pan and zoom a real world map to explore cities and regions
- Open city-to-city routes by clicking cities right on the map, each flown from a hub
- See every route drawn across the map, with cities colour-coded by trade pact
- Filter the map to the regions a trade pact's airlines can reach
- Set seat-class prices and freight rates, and watch each route's projected passenger and freight revenue

Run the business behind the routes:

- Keep the books in the General Ledger, with revenue and costs posted month by month
- View the ledger daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannually or annually
- Buy passenger and freighter jets from the Broker, or lease them and pay over the term
- Sell an aircraft, or cancel a lease and hand the airframe back to the Broker's Used Aircraft list
- Configure each cabin, from Ultra and First down to Main. Press Accept to save a seat count; the cabin seats together cannot exceed the aircraft's maximum
- Assign your fleet to routes within each aircraft's range. All its assigned flights share a 24-hour day, so route times and daily frequencies must fit
- Track pilots, cabin crew, mechanics, agents and administration in the Workforce tab. Staffing follows your schedule and wages follow your hub city
- Study each country and city's population, GDP and economy in the Locations tab

Play the wider world:

- Explore Trade Pacts by population, GDP, trade balance and economic sectors
- Mark countries as autarkic in GeoPolitical and compare imports, exports and passenger traffic
- Compare your airlines by routes, hubs and revenue in the Airlines tab
- Form an Alliance so member airlines can open routes in each other's regions; each member airline's CEO approves changes

Time runs on a game clock: each month that passes closes your books, and your loans and leases amortize a payment at a time. Set a game year to last anywhere from 15 minutes to 24 hours, for a quick game or a patient one.

Never lost? The Help tab explains every part of the game, from founding your airline to configuring cabin seats and assigning aircraft. The Information tab lets you send a message straight to the developer and read the responses you have received.

The Help tab guides you through the game; Information lets you contact the developer and read responses. Play on Android, Web or Windows.

Airline Investor is designed to be approachable and deep. Prices, distances and revenue are estimated for you, so you can focus on the decisions: which cities to connect, which aircraft to fly, and how to grow a small carrier into a worldwide airline. Your progress saves automatically, and every tab can be imported and exported as CSV if you want to inspect or edit the numbers yourself.

Start with one aircraft and one route - and see how far your airline can fly.
