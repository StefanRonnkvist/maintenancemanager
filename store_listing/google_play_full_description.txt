Keep maintenance and operational records organized with Maintenance Manager.

Use the management tabs to enter records and review them in tables:

- **Equipment** — track equipment category, business and location, local identifiers, manufacturer, model, serial number, maintenance manual, and publisher.
- **Spare Parts** — record OEM and vendor part details, quantity in stock, and storage location. Choose vendor names from businesses marked as Vendor.
- **People** — manage employee or contact details, phone numbers, email, business and work location, remote-work status, and nearest airport.
- **Business** — organize customer, vendor, and principle contact details, airports, and business locations.

Add forms are collapsible, so you can keep the records table in view while you work. Records are saved locally on your device and reload when you reopen the app.

Application DB provides editable local key/value records, plus CSV import and export for Equipment, Spare Parts, People, and Business. Select a category, then import a matching CSV, export its records, or save an example or blank template. Keep CSV exports as backups. Imported rows are added to the selected category.

Open Information for the Contact form and Submissions view. Contact sends your question, return contact details, and basic app/device diagnostics to the support service. Settings lets you choose System, Dark, or Light appearance. The Help tab includes in-app usage guidance.

Available for Android, Web, and Windows. Management and Application DB records are stored locally on the device; uninstalling or clearing app data may remove them.
